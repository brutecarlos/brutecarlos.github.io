Data we do not collect

Neither site has user accounts, forms, comments or a backend. We do not ask for or store your name, email address or any personal details.

Data stored in your browser (QAQEST)

QAQEST saves your exam attempts, scores and your ads preference in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device, is not sent to any server, and you can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads, which may be personalised based on your visits to this and other websites. On QAQEST, ads are opt-in: no ad code is loaded unless you choose "Support QAQEST with ads" in the header, and you can switch back to ad-free mode at any time.

Where required (European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland), you will be shown a consent message through a Google-certified consent management platform, and you can accept, decline or manage your choices.

Learn more about how Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

The sites are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address in server logs; see GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Pages may link to external sites (LinkedIn, GitHub and others). We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Your rights
